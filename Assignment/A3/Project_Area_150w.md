# Research Plan §1 — Project area (150 words)

## 正式版（可直接填进模板）

When a shared facility in an apartment building fails — a lift, a door, the
hot water — a queue forms that nobody living with it can see. Residents can
report the fault — to a landlord, an agent, a front desk, a caretaker. What
they cannot do is influence what happens next. Sequencing sits with operators,
providers and managers, and no legislation sets a timeframe: strata schemes owe
a strict repair duty (s 106(1), SSMA 2015 (NSW)), other landlords a "reasonable
state of repair" (s 63, RTA 2010 (NSW)). The wait is uneven, too: for a resident who cannot use
stairs, a lift outage removes access to their home.

I am not proposing to make repairs faster; capacity is fixed. I want to
investigate the **information and influence** around the wait: who is told,
who may ask, on what basis jobs are ordered, and whether that order can be
made visible.

**词数：150**

## 三处刻意的措辞

**"I am not proposing to make repairs faster — capacity is fixed."**
把 brief 的第一条约束写进 project area，等于告诉 tutor 你读懂了。不要省这句。

**"information and influence"**
brief 列出的合法杠杆里有 "with what information" 和 "in what order"。用它们的词，不要用 standing——租户是可以报修的，说他们"没有 standing"不准确。

**没有出现 "app"、"platform"、"system"**
一旦写死载体，A2c 的原型就被锁住了。留到访谈之后再定。
